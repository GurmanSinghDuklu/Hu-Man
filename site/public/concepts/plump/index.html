What we bake

01 / 07What we bake

Plump is a neighbourhood bakery in Sheffield. We make slow-proved bread every morning, buns and pastries to go with it, and celebration cakes to order.

Every day
Sourdough, tin loaves, rolls, croissants and buns
By the tray
Traybakes, tarts and sausage rolls for parties
To order
Birthday, tiered and wedding cakes
Flour
Unbleached, from a mill in Yorkshire
Where
Sheffield, South Yorkshire

We start the dough the afternoon before, so the bread has time to taste of something. Cakes are baked the day before collection, filled with real butter cream and finished by hand. If you have an allergy or a dietary need, tell us when you order and we will say honestly what we can and cannot do.

Notice needed

03 / 07How much warning we need

The longer the bake, the longer the notice. Each block is one day, and the bar stops at ten.

Sample notice, in days

  • Bread and rolls for collection 1 day

  • Boxes of buns and pastries 3 days

  • Traybakes and party savouries 3 days

  • Birthday cake, single tier 5 days

  • Cakes for allergies and diets 7 days

  • Tiered and wedding cakes 10 days or more

Sample lead times for this concept. Busy weekends and holidays may need longer.

How to order

05 / 07Three steps, usually done in two emails

Step 1

Tell us what you need

Date, number of guests, flavour

Email us with the day you need it and any allergies. Check the notice list first so the date is realistic.

Step 2

Agree the design and price

A quote within two working days

We send a short written quote and a sketch. A deposit holds the date for larger cakes.

Step 3

Collect on the day

Boxed and ready

Pick up from the shop at the time we agree. We pack the cake for the journey and tell you how to keep it.
