        How I work

        Plain, tidy and on time

        Moving between lots of workplaces taught me one thing: every business keeps its paperwork differently. I start by learning yours, then I make it boring in the best way.

        1. Look at the shoebox

          We go through what you have now: receipts, bank feeds, old spreadsheets. No judgement.

        2. Set it up once

          Accounts, categories and bank rules in Xero or whatever you already use, written down so anyone can follow them.

        3. Keep a monthly rhythm

          A fixed day each month for reconciling, chasing missing receipts and getting VAT ready.

        4. Report in plain English

          A one-page summary you can read in two minutes, and a call if something needs a decision.
