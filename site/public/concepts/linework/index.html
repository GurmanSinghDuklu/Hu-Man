How a project runs.

Stage 01Weeks 1 to 3

Brief

Site visit and survey

  • We visit, measure the building and listen to how you use it.
  • We agree a written brief, a rough budget and a sensible timeline.
  • We flag early anything that could change the plan, such as party walls or trees.

Stage 02Weeks 3 to 8

Design

Sketches to drawings

  • We sketch two or three layouts and talk them through with you.
  • We refine the chosen scheme with models, plans, sections and materials.
  • We agree the design with you before any application is made.

Stage 03Weeks 8 to 20

Planning

Permissions and detail

  • We prepare and submit the planning or permitted development paperwork.
  • We draw the technical details for building control and for builders to price.
  • We keep you posted while the council decides.

Stage 04On site

Build

Tender to handover

  • We help you choose a builder and compare their prices fairly.
  • We visit the site at key points and answer the builder's questions.
  • We walk the finished work with you and list anything to put right.
