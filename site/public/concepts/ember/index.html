The kitchen
in brief

Ember Street Kitchen is a small takeaway in Manchester that does two things properly: burgers and loaded fries. Everything is cooked to order on a hot flat-top, so nothing sits under a lamp waiting for you.

The menu is short on purpose. We would rather make five dishes well than thirty badly. We bag each order ourselves, check it against the ticket and hand it over, or send it out with a rider, while it is still hot.

  • Cooked to order. Patties hit the grill after you pay, not before.
  • Short menu. Five sections, a handful of dishes in each, and no filler.
  • Plain labelling. Every dish shows what is in it and which tags apply.
  • Honest timings. We quote a collection time and tell you if the queue grows.

    Good to know

    Short answers to the things people ask most. All sample entries for this concept.

    How long does collection take?
    Usually 15 to 20 minutes. We quote a time when you order and tell you if it changes.
    Can I change my order?
    Yes, until the kitchen starts cooking. Email us straight away with your order name.
    Is there a vegetarian choice?
    Yes. Look for the vegetarian and vegan tags on the menu, and ask us about cross-contact.
    Do you cater for groups?
    For six or more burgers, order a few hours ahead by email and we will agree a collection time.
    What if my order is wrong?
    Email us the same evening with your order name and we will put it right.
    Do you take bookings?
    No. We are a takeaway, so there are no tables, only a hatch and a delivery route. Ordering ahead by email is the closest thing we offer, and we are happy to agree a time that suits you both well.
