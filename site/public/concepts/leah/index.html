Approach®

Start to finish

  1. Step 01Understand

    What does done look like, who decides, and what can’t move? I get the brief, the budget and the constraints on one page.

  2. Step 02Plan

    Phases, owners and dates agreed with the people doing the work, with the risks listed from day one.

  3. Step 03Deliver

    A steady rhythm of check-ins, reports and decisions. Changes are priced and agreed before they happen.

  4. Step 04Hand over

    Snagging closed, documents in order, the team trained and the lessons written down.

Questions?

What size of project do you take on?

Fit-outs, public buildings and change programmes, usually with several teams and suppliers involved. If you’re not sure it fits, send a short note and I’ll say so honestly.

Can you work with our existing team and suppliers?

Yes. I’d rather join your way of working than replace it. I add the plan, the reporting rhythm and the registers if you don’t already have them.

How do you report progress?

A short written update on a set day each week, plus a one-page view for sponsors. What’s done, what’s next, what needs a decision.

Are you based in Bristol only?

Bristol is home. I travel for site work and run planning sessions remotely.

How do we start?

Email me. We’ll have a short call about the project, then I’ll send a plain outline of how I’d approach it.
