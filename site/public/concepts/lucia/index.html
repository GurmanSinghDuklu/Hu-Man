01 Profile

Plain
talk. Steady
accounts.

I am a B2B sales account manager in office supplies and facilities. I win new business from offices, schools, clinics and warehouses, and I keep the accounts I win by knowing how each customer buys.

I like a tidy pipeline: every account has an owner, a plan and a next step. I agree prices that work for both sides, flag problems early and hand over cleanly when a customer needs something from operations or finance.

Account growth · Healthcare · Sample account

Mere Valley Clinics

The challenge

Six clinics ordered separately, from different suppliers. Stock ran out at some sites and sat unused at others.

The approach

Visit each practice, list what they use, and agree one shared supply list and delivery day with the group's practice managers.

What I did

Wrote the account plan, set a quarterly review with the group, and trained the clinics on one simple reorder process.
