Main bio

I'm a customer service team leader who gets a bit too excited about making a busy contact centre feel calm.

I lead a team of advisers handling phone, email and chat for a home services company. I coach people one to one, plan the rota around real demand, and step in when a customer is upset and needs someone to listen first.

I like fixing the small things that cause big queues: a confusing script, a clumsy CRM field, a handover that always gets lost. I write them down, try a fix with the team and keep what helps.

Away from the desk I'm usually walking round Leicester with a flat white, or cooking for far too many people.

case study · Mar 2023

Refund scripts

Refund calls were where advisers sounded stiff and customers sounded cross. I rewrote the scripts with the team so a no sounds fair and a yes sounds quick.

Customer serviceTeam project
RoleTeam leader, project owner
TimelineSix weeks, started in spring
TeamFour advisers, one QA reviewer
ToolsZendesk, Genesys, Excel
Before and after: how a refund call opens
01

Listen to the calls

I sat in on refund calls with each adviser and wrote down the phrases that calmed people and the ones that didn't.

02

Write it together

The team drafted new wording in a shared document. We cut jargon and put the next step first.

03

Try it, then tidy it

Two advisers tested the scripts for a fortnight. We changed what felt stiff, then added them to Zendesk as macros.

what changed, in plain words:
AdvisersGet a clear first line for every refund call.
Quality reviewsNow include a kindness check.
New startersLearn the scripts in week one.
