About me

Floor-first.
Detail-minded.
Team-backed.

I'm Clara, a store manager in York. I started on the shop floor and I still spend most of my day there: coaching colleagues, walking the space with a customer's eyes and fixing the small things that make a store feel cared for.

I believe a good store is built from habits: tidy fixtures, honest advice, a team that knows why the numbers matter. Retail should be hard work that people enjoy.

How I lead

Five habits I keep, every shift.

Behind the counter

  1. Be on the floor.

    Most good decisions are made where the customers are. I start and end my day walking the store.

  2. Explain the why.

    A team that understands why a target or a rule matters will find better ways to meet it.

  3. Coach in the moment.

    Short, kind and specific feedback on the day beats a long review months later.

  4. Sweat the small things.

    Straight hangers, clean fitting rooms, a counted till. The details are what customers remember.

  5. Own the mistakes.

    When something goes wrong I say so, fix it with the team and change the routine so it doesn't repeat.
