Selected work

Rest on a project to see its screens.

01

Tally

A budgeting app for shift workersLead product designer, 2025
Problem
Pay lands on different days in different amounts, yet every budgeting app assumes a monthly salary. People on rotas were doing the sums on paper.
Outcome
Tally plans around the shift calendar instead of the month. Testers called it the first money app that didn't scold them for an uneven week.

02

Clinic Queue

Waiting room check-in for a fictional health trustUX and service designer, 2024
Problem
Reception was the bottleneck. People queued just to say their name, then sat unsure whether anyone knew they had arrived.
Outcome
Check-in takes one tap on a phone or kiosk, and the waiting screen says plainly where you are. Reception staff said it gave them time back for people who needed a human.

03

Loop Library

A community tool-lending appProduct designer, 2023
Problem
Most households own a drill they use twice a year. Neighbours would happily lend, but asking is awkward and nobody remembers who has what.
Outcome
Asking became easy and returning became easier. Pilot groups asked to keep it running after the trial ended.

04

Field Notes

A design system for a fictional outdoor brandDesign systems lead, 2022
Problem
Web, app and in-store screens had drifted into three different brands, and every new feature started with a debate about buttons.
Outcome
One small set of tokens and components that designers and developers both trusted. New screens started from the system instead of from a copy of the last one.

How I work

Four steps, in this order, every time.

  1. 1

    Listen

    I start in the room. Shadowing, interviews, and reading the support inbox before anyone opens Figma.

  2. 2

    Map

    The whole journey goes on one wall before I draw one screen. Gaps are cheaper to find on paper.

  3. 3

    Build

    I prototype fast enough to be wrong cheaply, in Figma or in code, whichever answers the question sooner.

  4. 4

    Test and ship

    Real people try it, I fix what confuses them, then I sit with the engineers until it is live.
