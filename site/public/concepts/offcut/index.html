What we do

Studio

We make good things from the leftovers of a brief: one clear idea, used well, everywhere it appears.

Offcut Studio is a small independent design studio in Leeds. We work on branding, packaging, print and websites for independent businesses, makers and community groups. You talk to the designers who make your work, not to an account manager.

Slide 04 / 08

How a project runs, sample timings

Process

  1. Week 1Step 01

    Discovery

    We learn how your business works.

    • Meet you and spend time where the work happens.
    • Collect what you already have, from old logos to receipts.
    • Agree a short written brief.
  2. Weeks 2 to 3Step 02

    Ideas

    We sketch widely before choosing.

    • Draw many rough options on paper.
    • Show two or three routes, each explained plainly.
    • Talk each one through with you before you choose.
  3. Weeks 4 to 6Step 03

    Design

    We build the chosen route properly.

    • Turn the idea into a full system of marks, colour and type.
    • Design the packaging, print or pages.
    • Test the work at real size and in real light.
  4. Week 7 onStep 04

    Handover

    We leave you ready to use it.

    • Prepare files for printers and developers.
    • Write a short guide to using the work.
    • Stay on call for your first launch.
