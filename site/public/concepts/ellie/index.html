About

Hello, I'm Ellie

I run Muddy Paws Walks (sample), a small dog walking and pet sitting service in Harrogate. I keep my diary small on purpose, so every dog gets proper attention and every owner knows who is coming through the door.

Before any booking we meet at your home. You tell me about your pet's routine, quirks and favourite treats, and I find out whether we suit each other. Then I write it all down, so nothing gets forgotten.

When I am not out walking, I am usually drying a towel or learning another dog's name.

Good to know

Questions

What happens at a meet-and-greet?

We meet at your home for about half an hour. I meet your pet, see where the lead and food live, and we agree a routine. It costs nothing and there is no obligation to book.

Where do you work?

Harrogate and the villages and neighbourhoods close around it. If you are not sure, send me your postcode and I will tell you.

How many dogs are in a group walk?

In this sample set-up, a small group, kept to dogs who get on well. Anyone who prefers their own company gets a solo walk instead.

Will I hear how the visit went?

Yes. After each visit you get a short message and a photo, so you know your pet is happy.

What if I need to cancel?

Let me know as early as you can. In this sample, 24 hours' notice means no charge.
