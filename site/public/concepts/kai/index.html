What I do

Four things I run.

  1. Guests

    Greeting, seating, the first drink and the last goodbye. I set the pace of a night so guests never feel the pressure behind it.

  2. Team

    A team of 40, from the door to the pass. Clear briefings, rotas that hold, and people who want the next shift.

  3. Stock and margin

    Ordering, wastage and GP looked at every week, so the numbers say something early, not at month end.

  4. New openings

    Hiring, training, a soft launch and a first service the whole team is ready for.

The route

From the door to the office.

  2. Years 3 to 5

    Supervisor

    Running a section, then the shift. First rotas, first difficult conversations.

  3. Years 6 to 8

    Assistant manager

    Ordering, cash-up, training plans and the weekly numbers.

    General manager

    The whole venue: guests, a team of 40, margin and what comes next.
