Chapter 01Diagnose

01 / Fault finding

Start with the complaint, not the code.

A fault code points at a circuit, not at a part. I confirm the problem, read the data, test the circuit and only then change anything. One change at a time, then retest.

Method

  1. 01.1Verify the complaintRoad test or reproduce it. Note the conditions: cold, hot, load, speed.
  2. 01.2Read the dataStored codes, freeze frame and live values from the scan tool.
  3. 01.3Test, don't guessMeter, scope, pressure and smoke tests before parts.
  4. 01.4Prove the fixRetest under the same conditions and record the result.

02 / Brakes, suspension, high voltage

Brakes that stop. Suspension that holds.

Parts are measured against the maker's limits, fitted clean and torqued to the figure in the book. On hybrid and electric cars the first job is always the same: isolate, prove dead, lock off, record.

Chapter 03Service

03 / Servicing, record, people

A service is an inspection with a schedule.

Oil and filters are the easy part. The value is in what gets measured, written down and flagged before it becomes a breakdown. Customers get a plain list: done, advised, can wait.

Every service sheet covers

  • Oil, filters and fluid levels
  • Brake wear, measured not guessed
  • Tyre depth, age and condition
  • Battery and charging test
  • Lights, wipers, washers
  • Suspension and steering joints
  • Open recalls and software updates
  • A written note of what to watch
