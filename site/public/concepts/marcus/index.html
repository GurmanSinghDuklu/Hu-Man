(01) Profilep. 02

I draw parts a machine shop can build without a phone call.

I'm a mechanical design engineer in Rotherham. Most of my nine years have been spent between the drawing office and the machine shop, turning a customer's idea into a model, a drawing and a set-up sheet the people at the machine can trust.

I work in SolidWorks every day and review designs for manufacture early. I care about the unglamorous details that stop a job: datum choices, tolerances, workholding and the order of operations.

Away from the screen I run improvement projects on the floor and write the risk assessments that keep them safe.

(04) Safetyp. 11

Safe is a design input, not a sign on the wall.

  • S-01

    Risk assessments written with the operators, and reviewed when the job changes.

  • S-02

    Guarding and interlocks designed in from the first model, not added at install.

  • S-03

    Clear rules for PPE, walkways and lifting, shown where people actually work.

  • S-04

    Near misses logged, talked through at the start-up meeting and turned into changes.
