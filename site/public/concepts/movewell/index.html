Where it hurts

Pick the part that's sore.

Each one tells you what we usually see and how a first session goes.

Lower back

Stiffness after sitting, or pain that runs into the leg. Often from desk work, lifting or a sudden twist.

  • We check how you move and where it catches
  • Hands-on treatment to ease it
  • Three or four exercises to start at home
