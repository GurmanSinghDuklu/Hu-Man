Ferry

A job queue that survives restarts

ferry / lease and recover
Role
Sole author, design to rollout
Stack
Go, PostgreSQL, Prometheus
Year
2025

Problem

Overnight import jobs vanished whenever a worker restarted mid-run. The team heard about it from customers, not from alerts.

What I built

A queue that writes each job to an append-only log before acknowledging it, hands work out on short heartbeat leases, and replays anything unfinished at boot. Retries back off with jitter, and a dead-letter lane can be inspected and re-driven by hand.

Outcome

Deploys stopped being scheduled around the queue. Restarts became routine, and a lost job turned into something the team had to be reminded was ever possible.

Ledgerline

A double-entry payments service

ledger / transfer.kt
fun transfer(from, to, amount, key) { - from.balance -= amount - to.balance += amount + ledger.post(key, listOf( + debit(from, amount), + credit(to, amount), + )) + check(entries.sum() == 0) } balances are derived, never stored
Role
Backend lead, team of four
Stack
Kotlin, PostgreSQL, Kafka
Year
2023 to 2024

Problem

Balances lived in a single mutable column. Untangling a disputed payment meant reading logs and making an educated guess.

What I built

An immutable ledger where every movement is a balanced pair of entries committed in one transaction. Every write carries an idempotency key, and balances are computed from entries rather than stored.

Outcome

Finance could answer "where did this money go" with a query instead of an investigation. Disputes became lookups, and retries stopped being scary.

Pathfinder

A routing engine for Marrow & Finch Couriers

pathfinder / reroute
Role
Backend engineer, owned the solver service
Stack
Rust, PostGIS, gRPC
Year
2022 to 2023

Problem

Dispatchers rebuilt each day's routes by hand in spreadsheets, and every late order broke the plan they had just finished.

What I built

A graph-based engine that honours delivery windows, precomputes road distances up front, and re-plans only the affected stretch when an order arrives. Each stop carries a short reason for why it sits where it does.

Outcome

Dispatchers went from drawing routes to approving them. Late orders slot in without a full rebuild, and the "why is this stop here" question answers itself.

tiny-diff

Open source: a text diff library

tiny-diff / diff("the quick brown fox", ...)
beforethe quick brown fox
afterthe quick red fox jumps
edit script= the= quick- brown+ red= fox+ jumps
Role
Author and maintainer
Stack
TypeScript, no dependencies
Since
2021

Problem

Showing what changed in a form field shouldn't need a diff library bigger than the form. The options were all heavier than the job.

What I built

A token-level Myers diff in a few hundred lines. It tree-shakes cleanly, returns a plain edit script, and prints output a person can read.

Outcome

Small enough to read in an evening. Contributors have found their way around it without a guide, which is the review I care about most.
