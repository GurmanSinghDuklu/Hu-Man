Priya Nair

Operations manager

Nine years running warehouse and distribution operations. Follow the dot.

A sample week's rota

I'm Priya. I run the bit of the business nobody sees until it goes wrong: the rota that makes Monday work, the stock count that finally matches the shelf, and the quiet fix that stops Friday's problem turning up again next Friday.

Nine years on warehouse floors have taught me that good operations is mostly listening, then writing it down so it sticks. I like calm shifts, honest numbers and people who know what happens next.

I run

  1. rotas

    Shifts that cover the peaks, respect people's lives and don't fall over when someone's off sick.

  2. warehouses

    Safe, tidy floors where stock is where the system says it is and nobody hunts for a pallet.

  3. the numbers

    Stock accuracy, picking errors, overtime: counted every week, shared plainly, acted on.

  4. the room

    Team briefs, tough conversations and handovers. The people part is the job.

How I work

Rota first.

Most problems start as a staffing problem. I plan the people, then the process, and leave slack for the day it all changes.

Count it twice.

If the system and the shelf disagree, I go and look. Small, regular counts beat one big surprise a year.

Write it down.

A fix that lives in one person's head isn't a fix. Short checklists, plain handovers, and a note for the next shift.
