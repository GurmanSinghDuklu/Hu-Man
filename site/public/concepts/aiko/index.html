Hello, I’m Aiko.

I have spent my career in commercial buildings, from small offices to multi-tenant sites. My work sits between people, plant and paperwork: planning maintenance, running safety checks and managing the contractors who do the hands-on jobs.

I like calm, well-documented sites where problems are found early. I write clear handovers, keep asset registers honest and talk to everyone from the board to the night cleaner.
