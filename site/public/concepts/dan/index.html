02 / About

From labourer to site manager

  • SMSTS
  • CSCS card
  • First aid at work
  • Temporary works awareness

I started on housing sites carrying and mixing, and learned the job from the ground up. Twelve years on, I run the site: who is on which floor this week, what has to be signed off before the next trade starts, and what the numbers say before they become a surprise.

I like jobs where the programme is tight and the site is busy. A clear look-ahead, a proper daily briefing and subcontractors who know what is expected of them do more for a project than heroics in week thirty.

  1. 2014LabourerHousing sites. Learned materials, setting out and how a site really runs.
  2. 2017ChargehandFirst crews of my own. First time owning a daily plan.
  3. 2020Assistant site managerSchools and extensions, working alongside the site manager on programme and permits.
  4. 2022Site managerHousing and retail fit-outs from start on site to handover.

Programme

Weekly look-ahead plans, a three-week lookahead for each trade, and one honest list of what is holding the critical path.

Health and safety

Briefings, permits and inspections done every day, not just before an audit. Near misses recorded and talked about.

Subcontractors

Clear scopes, early conversations about access and sequence, and a fair view of what each trade needs to finish properly.

Cost control

Measured progress against the budget each week, variations logged as they happen and cash flow kept in view.
