About me03

Pay ref IL-0726-03Period 07

I make sure people are paid the right amount, on the right day, every time.

I am a payroll administrator in Brighton with eight years in small and mid-sized teams. I run weekly, monthly and four-weekly pay cycles, keep records tidy for HMRC and pensions providers, and answer the questions staff are often too nervous to ask. I like clear checklists, quiet month-ends and payslips nobody has to query.

I am happiest when the process is documented well enough that anyone could pick it up. I look for a role where accuracy and a friendly tone both matter.
