The practice

A small practice, unhurried appointments.

We keep the surgery quiet, the explanations plain and the diary a little looser than most.

Fernlea is an independent practice in York, run by a small team who like to see familiar faces. We explain what we see, talk through the options and leave the decision with you.

Whether you come for a routine check-up or for something that has been worrying you for a while, you will not be rushed or lectured.

Treatments

Five kinds of appointment, plainly put.

Your dentist will talk you through what suits you before anything starts.

  1. 01

    Check-ups

    A routine look at your teeth and gums, with time to ask questions and agree how often to come back.

  2. 02

    Hygienist visits

    A thorough clean and friendly advice on looking after your teeth and gums at home.

  3. 03

    Fillings

    Repairing a tooth that has a cavity or a chip, explained step by step before we begin.

  4. 04

    Whitening

    A cosmetic option for those who would like lighter teeth. We talk through what is and is not suitable.

  5. 05

    Emergency appointments

    Sudden pain or a broken tooth. We hold time free each day for urgent problems, so get in touch as early as you can.

Your first visit

Four steps, no surprises.

This is what usually happens, in order. Ask us if you would like anything done differently.

Step one
Before you comeBooking

Get in touch

By email, at a time that suits you

  • Send a short message with the days that work best for you.
  • Tell us if you feel nervous or have worries about a particular treatment.
  • If you like, we will send a short health form to fill in at home.
Step two
On the dayArriving

Arrive and settle

A step-free entrance and a quiet waiting area

  • Arrive a few minutes early so there is no rush.
  • Meet the person looking after you and ask anything you like.
  • Take the time you need. Nobody will hurry you.
Step three
In the chairCheck-up

Your check-up

Unhurried, with plain explanations

  • Your dentist looks at your teeth and gums and asks about your health history.
  • Anything we find is explained in plain words, with the options set out.
  • Nothing starts without your agreement.
Step four
Before you goAfterwards

Plan the next step

Written down, so you do not have to remember

  • We note what was discussed and give you a copy.
  • We suggest when to come back, and you can book before you leave.
