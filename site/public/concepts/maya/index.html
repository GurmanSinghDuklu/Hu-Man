Hi, I'm Maya. I make small brands easy to pass along.

I'm a content creator and social media marketer in London. I write, film and cut short-form video for shops, studios and independents who have a good thing and no budget for a big agency.

Most of my work starts with one question: what would someone send to the group chat? Then I build the idea, the videos and the habits around it, so the account keeps going after the launch week.

I'm a team of one, sometimes with an editor friend. You get me on every call.
