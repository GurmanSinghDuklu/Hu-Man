## What the 25% cut means
At mid-range hours the introductory prices earn about £27 to £30 an hour (Launch about £30, Growth about £27, Full presence about £29). That is slightly below typical junior freelance rates of roughly £30 to £45 an hour. It is a deliberate investment in reputation, so scope discipline matters: capped pages and revisions, content deadlines, and bespoke work priced separately.

## Public wording

## Terms to reflect in the contract and on the site
Fixed price after a free discovery call; 50% deposit and the rest before launch or handover; two revision rounds included; the client owns the site and content after final payment; model, API and platform fees billed at cost; content deadlines set at kick-off.

## Price-rise ladder (suggested)
Review prices every five completed projects. Raise by 10% to 15% at a review if the diary is booked more than about six weeks ahead or more than half of proposals are accepted. Keep monthly fees stable unless the service scope changes.
