# Brand-voice copy draft (for sign-off)

Status: draft, 2026-10-13. Nothing here is on the site yet. Once Gurman approves (or edits) it,
`docs/03-site-spec.md` is updated first, then the components and `src/data/content.ts`.

Rules applied (docs/brand/1-brand.md plus the project's hard rules): "we" and "you"; headlines 3 to
8 words (set in capitals by CSS, written in sentence case); the slash once per piece at most; no hype,
no exclamation marks, no guarantees; prices, credentials and facts unchanged. The chat demo replies stay
as they are, because they speak for the fictional barbershop, not for Hu/man.

## Decisions needed first

1. **"We" for a one-person studio.** The brand voice uses "we". That's normal for a business, but the
   site currently says "A one-person studio" and "I design, build and test". Draft below uses "we"
   throughout and drops the "one-person" headline. Alternative: keep "I" everywhere.
2. **Main button.** The brand's example is "Book a call", but there's no booking tool, and the site's
   job is the free audit. Draft keeps "Get your free audit" as the primary button and uses "Book a
   call" (a phone link) as a secondary one in the audit section.
3. **Tagline.** Draft adds the brand line "Your human in the loop" as the hero label.
4. **Case studies.** The Calculator App and Essence were built by Gurman before the studio existed.
   Draft says "What we built" for consistency. Say if you'd rather keep "What I built" there.
