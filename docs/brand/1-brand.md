# Brand

## The idea

**Your human in the loop.** AI can build a page in seconds; it cannot pick up the phone when the page breaks. Hu/man is the person on the other end: a real builder who makes small businesses findable by Google and AI search, and stays.

The slash in the name splits HU and MAN the way a fingerprint splits into ridges: the human, with a cut of technology running through it.

## What we stand for

| Value | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Human | A named person answers. We show the client their new site before we ask for anything. |
| Honest | We say what AI search can and can't do. No guarantees of rankings or AI citations. |
| Staying | Fixes are part of the deal. We don't disappear after launch. |
| Sharp | Modern, fast, well built. The work looks like it came from a much bigger studio. |

## Voice

We talk like a capable friend who builds websites: plain, direct, warm, a bit dry. Short sentences. British English.

- **Do**: lead with what the client gets ("Your site, rebuilt and found"). Use "we" and "you". Name real things: Google Business Profile, ChatGPT, AI Overviews.
- **Don't**: hype ("revolutionary", "next-level"), urgency ("act now!"), promises we can't keep ("#1 on Google", "guaranteed"), exclamation marks, emoji.
- **Headlines**: 3 to 8 words, set in capitals. The slash may join two ideas once per piece: SEO/GEO, WEB/AI, BUILD/STAY.
- **Labels**: mono capitals, 1 to 5 words, often with a slash or a middle dot as separator.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock your digital potential! | Your site, rebuilt and found. |
| We leverage AI-driven SEO solutions | We make Google and ChatGPT understand your business. |
| 24/7 world-class support | If something breaks, call us. We fix it. |
| Contact us today! | Book a call |

## Naming

- The brand is **Hu/man**: capital H, slash, lowercase man. In running text: "Hu/man". In display capitals: "HU/MAN".
- Never "Human", "HU-MAN" or "Hu man" as the brand name. In a URL or handle where the slash can't go, use the agreed handle (not yet chosen).
- The giveaway is **the Hu/man Gift**. The partner programme is **Hu/man Founding Partners**.
