# Applications

## Social

The feed should look like one poster wall. Every post uses the 4 × 5 grid, the palette and the type; the variety comes from which modules and which cells.

- **Rhythm** across a 3-wide feed: alternate Ink-led and Paper-led posts so the grid reads as a checkerboard from a distance.
- **Templates**: Announcement, Carousel cover, Quote (see SocialPost). Add a Before/After template once real client numbers exist.
- **Captions**: first line is the headline in plain words; one call to action; no more than 3 hashtags; `#ad` on any Founding Partner post.
- **Profile picture**: the Ridge print in Ink on Signal, so the avatar reads as a fingerprint at 110 px. The full wordmark is unreadable at that size.

## Business cards

Ink front with the gradient logo; Paper back with the Ridge print on Signal, name, role and three contact lines. Full spec in BusinessCard. Order a proof before any run.

## Posters and flyers

A-series portrait on the 4 × 6 grid (see ModularPoster). The quarterly **Hu/man Gift** poster always uses its edition as the numeral (1/4, 2/4, 3/4, 4/4) and Pulse as its one extra colour.

## Website

- Dark (`surface` Ink) by default; documents and long reads may use the Paper theme.
- The hero is a band of modules: logo or headline in one block, Ridge field and two pattern modules beside it. Not a full-screen photo.
- Body copy in `body-l` and `body`, at most 70 characters a line.
- Primary action `hm-btn--primary` ("Book a call"); one per screen.
- Sections are separated by `line` hairlines and `space-24`, never by background gradients.

## Presentations

Use the Slides artifact with this system: Ink slides, Montserrat capitals, an 8-column module band at the top or side of key slides, mono rails for page numbers.

## Email signature

The 600 × 100 px strip already made (logo, tagline, services line on Ink) sits under a plain-text name, role and contact lines.

## Founding Partner kit

Each partner gets their own Announcement post, with their business name in the Paper block and their own photo in the field cell, plus a story frame. Same grid, their story.

## Checklist before anything goes out

1. Is it on the module grid, with one thing per cell?
2. Is there a Ridge or ring module?
3. Is there one Signal block and at most one Pulse block?
4. Is the headline 8 words or fewer, in capitals?
5. Are all diagonals at 68°?
6. Is every text and ground pair from the contrast table in Identity?
7. Is the logo one of the four versions, at or above minimum size?
