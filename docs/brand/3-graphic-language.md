# Graphic language

## The visual DNA, from the moodboard

| Reference | What we take | What we leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAVIS event posters | The modular grid of square cells; op-art rings, squares and hexagons; huge clipped numerals; vertical "Book now" rails; black-and-white photos in single cells | Nothing: their violet and orange are now ours (Pulse and Flare) |
| VORTEX techno posters | Thin line patterns, starbursts, checkers and quarter-arcs as modules; one colour plus black and white; arrows | The busy 7-column density |
| MTS social grid | A recognisable feed: every post shares grid, palette and type; black-and-white people with colour blocks over them; pattern cut by photography | The red; collage clutter and torn paper |
| Super Fomo VHS packs | Retro-tech labels: product codes, mono specs, bold numerals | The cassette nostalgia |
| HU/MAN fingerprint logo | The Ridge: fingerprint lines as the brand's own pattern | |

The name for the style: **neo-Swiss brutalism**. Swiss modular grid and type discipline, brutalist hard edges and raw blocks, op-art geometry, finished with tech-minimal restraint.

## The module grid

Every layout is a flush grid of square modules, no gutters, cells touching.

| Format | Grid | Module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed post 1080 × 1350 | 4 × 5 | 270 px |
| Square post 1080 × 1080 | 4 × 4 | 270 px |
| Story 1080 × 1920 | 4 × 7 (top and bottom 250 px kept clear) | 270 px |
| A-series poster | 4 × 6 | width ÷ 4 |
| Business card 85 × 55 mm | 6 × 4 | about 14 mm |
| Slide 1920 × 1080 | 8 × 4.5, used as 8 × 4 plus a footer band | 240 px |
| Web | 12 columns; hero bands built from 4 × 2 modules | fluid |

Merge cells into 1 × 2, 2 × 1 or 2 × 2 blocks. Each cell holds one thing: a pattern, a photo, a numeral, a headline, a label or the logo. Content inside a cell sits on `space-6` padding (scale it with the format).

## The Ridge

The fingerprint is the brand's signature: identity that can't be faked. The ridges are drawn as long, unbroken lines with only a few ridge endings, so they read as a real fingertip rather than a dotted pattern. It comes in three forms:
- **Ridge print** (`ridge-print-*.svg`): a single fingertip, centred in a square cell. Ink on Signal, Signal on Ink, Paper on Ink.
- **Ridge field** (`ridge-field-*.svg`): ridge lines edge to edge, for large backgrounds only. In a small cell it stops reading as a fingerprint; use the print there.
- **Ridge logo** (`logo-ridge.jpg`): the wordmark filled with ridges, for hero moments.

The ring, square and hex modules are the Ridge made geometric. Every layout contains at least one Ridge or ring module.

## The slash

The slash from the logo, at 68° from horizontal, is the only diagonal. It appears as the `slash` and `stripes` modules, as a cut through a block, and in copy (SEO/GEO, WEB/AI). Never rotate it or draw a diagonal at another angle.

## Photography

- Black and white only, high contrast, natural grain. Colour enters through the blocks around a photo, never inside it.
- Real subjects: Gurman at work, hands on keyboards and phones, clients in their own shops, fingertips and screens close up.
- Crop tight into a single cell or a 2 × 2 block. People may be cut out on Paper, as in the MTS posts.
- Optional duotone: shadows `brand-ink`, highlights `brand-paper`, mid-tones `concrete`.
- Never: stock handshakes, laptops on clean desks, smiling team shots, AI-generated people.

## Motion

Modules snap, they don't float. Cells cut on and off in a grid sequence (80 to 120 ms per cell); rings and ridges may rotate slowly (one turn in 20 s or more). No bounces or springs. Respect reduced-motion settings.

## Iconography

No icon set. Arrows are text (↗ only, for links that leave the page). Status uses words plus a filled or empty dot (● ○). Where a symbol is needed, use a module or a mono label instead.
