## Positioning
Sell outcomes, not "AI" or "2026": more bookings, fewer missed enquiries, quotes sent faster, bills paid sooner, and being found when customers search Google or ask an AI assistant. One line: "Websites that turn local searches into bookings."

Pick one or two sectors to start with so templates and case studies compound. Candidates in order of my starting bet (interviews should override it):
1. Trades (electricians, builders): fast sales, owner decides alone, admin and missed calls are the pain.
2. Hair, beauty and health practices (dental, physio): higher budgets and value per customer, more regulation and risk (health data), slower sales.
3. Takeaways and restaurants: strong pain (calls at peak, delivery-app commission of 15% to 35%) but price-sensitive.
4. Franchise owners: best scaling lever, because one win can repeat across their sites.

Gurman has access to owners in all of these (takeaways, dental practices, physiotherapists, electricians, builders, restaurants, franchise owners). Use that access first for 15 to 20 short discovery conversations before choosing.
